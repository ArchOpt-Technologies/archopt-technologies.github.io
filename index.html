PareVia NSGA-III · Pareto front

Building Energy Optimization Software for EnergyPlus

PareVia is a building design optimization software that uses EnergyPlus, XGBoost surrogate modelling, and NSGA-III multi-objective optimization to identify high-performance building designs based on energy use, embodied carbon, cost, and other performance objectives.
