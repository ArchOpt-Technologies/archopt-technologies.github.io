What EnergyFront V3.5 offers

Detailed geometry, run-time control through the EnergyPlus API, and AI-assisted scripting, on top of the full model-building workflow.

Floor by floorEach level can have its own plan, floor count and space types, with a plan editor for every level.
Windows, wall by wallPlace windows on each individual wall and see them on the wall before the IDF is generated.
Advanced HVAC controlOperative temperature thermostat control, overcooling to dehumidify, heat recovery and free-cooling options.
Control logic every time stepPython control scripts run through the EnergyPlus API and change setpoints, schedules or actuators during the simulation.
Every decision loggedEach control action is written to a log file, so the behaviour can be checked hour by hour.
Read, decide, actTest IoT and smart-building strategies such as adaptive EN 16798-1 setpoints or pre-cooling before occupancy.
AI-assisted scriptsDescribe the control or analysis in plain words, let an AI assistant write the script, then check and run it.
Your own analysisCustom result scripts for your own objectives, such as occupied hours above a comfort limit by month and zone.
Help written for engineersEvery page explains its inputs and how each result is calculated.
