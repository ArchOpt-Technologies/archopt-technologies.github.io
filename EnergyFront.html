WORKFLOW

Four steps from a building description to results

1

Describe the building

Choose the building type, draw or enter the floor plans, set the levels and space types. Zoning is created automatically.

2

Set envelope and systems

Define constructions layer by layer, glazing, HVAC, domestic hot water, natural ventilation, and schedules.

3

Generate and run

EnergyFront writes the IDF and runs EnergyPlus, with a live progress panel that reports each simulation step and any failure reason.

4

Review and export

Inspect energy, loads, zone conditions, comfort, and overheating heat maps, then export the results to Excel and take the IDF into PareVia.

CAPABILITIES

The inputs a detailed energy model needs, in one interface

📐

Geometry and zoning

Perimeter and core zoning, zone subdivision, freely drawn plans, and multi-level buildings where each level can have its own plan and use. Building types include Villa / Single Detached House.

🧱

Layer-by-layer envelope

Light, Medium, and Heavy thermal mass options with real layered constructions, layered glazing assemblies, doors, and a 3D view of the construction layers.

❄️

HVAC and hot water

Seven HVAC system types generated through EnergyPlus HVACTemplate objects, plus domestic hot water.

🌬️

Natural ventilation

Two modelling levels: a simple scheduled air change rate, or the full EnergyPlus Airflow Network.

🗓️

Flexible schedules

Annual, seasonal, monthly, and weekly patterns, with file import and export and a template for generating schedule files with an AI assistant.

🌡️

Comfort and overheating

PMV / PPD and overheating hours by static threshold, ASHRAE 55 adaptive, EN 16798-1 adaptive, and PMV / PPD criteria, shown per zone with heat maps.

THE SUITE WORKFLOW

EnergyFront comes first

EnergyFront builds and simulates the EnergyPlus model. PareVia then optimizes the design across energy, embodied carbon, and cost, and EPCalibration calibrates a model against measured data. EnergyFront is a separate product and does not require PareVia.
