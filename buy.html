🎓 Proof of student status (required)

Student licenses are issued only to currently enrolled students. After purchase, please send an official confirmation from your university or your supervisor stating that you are currently a student. For example:

  • An enrollment or status letter issued by your university, or
  • A signed letter or email from your supervisor on university letterhead or from a university email address

The document should show your full name, the university, your programme, and the current academic year. Student licenses are for non-commercial academic and research use only.

🤝 Academic and research collaboration

Students working on building energy performance, optimization, or net-zero topics may have opportunities for additional academic or research collaboration where appropriate, for example on joint publications, case studies, or methodological development. If this interests you, mention your research topic and supervisor in your application.

Common questions
Choose the Student License only if you are currently enrolled at a university and will use PareVia for non-commercial academic or research work. For any work for clients, consultancy, or use within a company, choose the Commercial License.
An official confirmation from your university or your supervisor stating that you are currently a student, such as an enrollment letter or a signed letter or email from your supervisor. It should show your name, university, programme, and the current academic year.
The introductory prices cover the first 3 months or the first year only. After that, your license continues at the standard rate for your category: Student $40/month or $300/year, Commercial $100/month or $1,000/year. If you do not renew, PareVia shows a renewal screen when the license expires.
A Student License remains valid until the end of its paid period. Renewals at student rates require current student status. After graduation, please move to a Commercial License for any professional use.
Each license is bound to a single machine. If you change computers, contact us and we will reset your license. For several machines, ask us for a multi-seat quotation.
Commercial keys are emailed after payment is confirmed, normally within 24 hours. Student keys are issued after payment is complete and your student status has been verified.
We offer a 7-day refund if PareVia does not work on your machine and we are unable to fix it. Contact us by email within 7 days of purchase. Please download the free installer and confirm it runs on your machine before buying.
