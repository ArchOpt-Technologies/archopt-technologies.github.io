Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 May 2026 · ArchOpt Technologies

1. Payment data

Payments are processed by PayPal, which collects your name, email, and payment details to deliver your receipt and license key. We never see your card or account details.

2. Data collected by the software

  • A hashed hardware fingerprint (SHA-256) used solely to bind your license to your machine — raw identifiers are never stored or transmitted.
  • License activation status and date, cached locally at %APPDATA%.

3. What we do not collect

We do not access your project files, simulation inputs, IDF files, or results. No usage analytics, no cookies on this site, no data sold to third parties.

4. Retention

License activation data is retained for the license period plus 90 days.
